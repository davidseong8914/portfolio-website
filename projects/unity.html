Virtual Infantry Training Simulation - Unity

Overview

Developed an immersive digital simulation of the Nonsan Training Ground, featuring a detailed landscape and comprehensive training exercises. Utilized a physics engine to ensure realistic interactions and dynamics within the environment. Integrated easy keyboard based player control mechanisms to enhance user experience and training effectiveness.

Gameplay

Nonsan Training Ground simulation is a multi-stage fps style program where users can move, jump, crawl and perform various actions to avoid obstacles and get to the end.
