Book Case

Book case to protect books from damage during transportation. It has a screw to support different book thicknesses and width, length large enough to hold more than 95% of the books I have.

Gauntlet JBL Speaker Cover

Gauntlet shaped JBL speaker cover. This 3d printed model will leverage the colorful lights from the JBL Pulse 4 speaker to mimic Gauntlet's infinity gems serving not only as a replica but a night lamp.

Sisyfos - Desktop Study Mate

Inspired by Sisyphus, the Greek mythological king condemned to an eternal struggle, this desktop object serves as both a toy and a motivational tool. When the user places a boulder in its hands, it begins to function, periodically offering motivational quotes to motivate and encourage the user.
